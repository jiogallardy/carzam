Privacy Policy

Effective May 14, 2026

Carzam (the "App") identifies cars by their engine sound. This page explains what data the App collects, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Audio recordings. When you tap the Carzam button, the app records ~5 seconds of audio at 16 kHz mono and uploads it to our server for classification. The clip is stored so the audio model can be improved.
  • Account identifier. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we store the unique identifier they give us (an opaque string), plus the email and display name they choose to share. We never see your password.
  • Your "garage." Cars you add (year, make, model, trim, color, notes) are stored on our server tied to your account.
  • Predictions and discoveries. The model's outputs (which car classes you have heard, with what confidence) are stored so your collection persists across devices.

What we do NOT collect

  • Location data.
  • Contacts, photos, calendars, or other personal content.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID). No third-party trackers.
  • Continuous background audio — recording is explicit, 5 seconds at a time, after a tap.

How we use it

  • App functionality. Classifying what you record and showing the result.
  • Model improvement. Audio clips you submit (especially "Add as new car" submissions) are reviewed and may be added to future training datasets. Clips tied to your account ID are pseudonymous — your name and email are not associated with individual clips when the dataset is processed.
  • Account management. Your sign-in identifier is used to authenticate you and tie your data to you.

Who we share it with

Nobody, with these exceptions:

  • Cloudflare R2 — stores audio clips. They process the bytes but cannot access them without our credentials.
  • Hetzner Cloud — hosts our API and database in Germany or the United States.
  • Apple & Google — only for the OAuth handshake when you sign in. They don't receive your audio.

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not share it with advertisers.

Your rights

  • Delete your account. Email privacy@carzam.app with the subject "Delete my account" from the email address tied to your account. We delete your user record, garage, predictions, discoveries, and all audio clips you submitted within 30 days.
  • Export your data. Same email, subject "Export my data." We send a JSON dump of everything tied to your account within 30 days.
  • Stop sending clips. Audio is only uploaded when you tap the Carzam button. Don't tap it and nothing leaves the device.

Children

Carzam is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we'll post the new version here and update the "Effective" date above. Material changes will also be announced in-app.

Contact
