How it works.

Three taps. The model does the rest.

1

Tap to listen.

One button. Five-second recording at 16 kHz. Hold the phone near the engine bay or exhaust.

2

The model thinks.

A CNN14 audio backbone trained on supercar and tuner clips classifies the engine family in ~200 ms.

3

Collect them all.

Every match unlocks the car in your "Found" collection. Like Shazam for engines, with a Pokédex.

It knows the difference.
